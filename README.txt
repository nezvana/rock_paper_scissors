/**
 * Rock, Paper, Scissors
 *
 * Brief:
 *   A simple two-player hand game where each player simultaneously chooses one of three gestures:
 *   Rock, Paper, or Scissors.
 *
 * Rules:
 *   - Rock beats Scissors (rock crushes scissors).
 *   - Scissors beats Paper (scissors cut paper).
 *   - Paper beats Rock (paper covers rock).
 *   - If both players choose the same gesture, the round is a tie.
 *
 * Gameplay:
 *   - Players reveal their choices at the same time.
 *   - A round winner is determined by the rules above.
 *   - Matches often use a "best of N" or first-to-X-wins scoring system; tied rounds typically do not count
 *     toward victory and may be replayed.
 *
 * Uses:
 *   - Decision-making (random tie-breaker), simple competitive play, learning probabilistic/opponent-pattern strategies.
 *
 * Variants:
 *   - Common extensions add more gestures (e.g., Rock, Paper, Scissors, Lizard, Spock) with an adjusted
 *     outcome matrix.
 */
